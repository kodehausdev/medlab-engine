What she does — and what she'll never do

The guardrails are the product.

Generic AI answering bots point a chatbot at a clinic and hope. Kesa was built for medical calls from the first line of code — which means the "never" list below is enforced by architecture, not by asking the AI nicely.

Kesa does

  • Answer every call and text instantly, 24/7, holidays included
  • Book appointments against your live schedule and capacity
  • Reschedule and cancel — for the verified caller only
  • Answer hours, prep, and fasting instructions per test
  • Text confirmations and reminders
  • Take messages and page your staff when it matters

Kesa never

  • Gives test results — those always come from your humans
  • Gives medical advice or plays triage
  • Continues past distress — callers hear a 911/ER redirect immediately, and your staff are paged
  • Pretends to be human — she discloses she's AI on every call
  • Texts anyone who opted out
  • Stores conversations — the only thing saved is the appointment

Every "never" is enforced at the system level, below the AI. Bring your compliance person — we'll happily walk them through the architecture.

Questions

Asked on every call.

What happens when a patient asks for results or medical advice?

Kesa takes a message and your staff calls back. Results and medical questions always come from humans — she'll say so warmly and offer what she can do instead, like booking the follow-up. It's a hard boundary enforced below the AI, not a preference.

How do you handle patient privacy?

Kesa is built for HIPAA-grade handling from the ground up: conversations are never stored, the only durable patient data is the appointment itself, logs are auto-redacted, and every AI action passes a compliance guard before it executes. We're happy to walk your compliance officer through the architecture — most vendors dodge that meeting; we invite it.

Do we have to change our phone system?

No — that's the point. Kesa works on the number your patients already call, via forwarding. No porting, no VoIP migration, no retraining your team.

What if Kesa can't handle something?

She takes a message, notes it for your staff, and it appears in your dashboard instantly. If a caller asks for a human, she hands off gracefully — and if the system is ever unreachable, calls simply forward per your fallback rules. She's designed to fail toward humans.

What does setup involve?

You sign up (card saved, $0 charged), tell us about your practice, and we configure everything white-glove — your tests, hours, chairs, prep rules, and who gets paged. Go-live is a deliberate decision we make together, and that's when billing starts. Typically days, not minutes.
